# 倒装句
**一、完全倒装**  
定义：谓语全部放在主语前。  
结构：谓语 + 主语

1. 常见结构

| 类型 | 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① 副词开头 | 副词 + 谓语 + 主语 | Here **comes** the bus.   Out **rushed** the children.   Now **comes** the time. |
| ② 地点介词短语开头 | 介词短语 + 谓语 + 主语 | On the wall **hangs** a map.   Under the tree **sits** an old man. |
| ③ 表语前置 | 形容词/过去分词/现在分词/介词短语 + be + 主语 | Present at the meeting **were** many scientists.   Gone **are** the days.   Standing at the door **was** a girl. |
| ④ there be 句型 | There + be/live/stand... + 主语 | There **is** a book on the desk.   There **stands** a temple on the hill. |


2. 标志
+ **there, here, down, up, in, out, now, then, before, away, off** 等副词开头，表示强调时用完全倒装。
+ **地点介词短语作状语位于句首**时，常用完全倒装。
+ 谓语多为 **be / lie / stand / sit / hang / come / go** 等。
3. 注意**主语为人称代词时不倒装**：  
    - Here **he comes**.  
    - Out **they rushed**.

**as/though 引导让步状语从句**不是完全倒装，应单列：  
结构：**表语/状语/动词原形 + as/though + 主语 + 谓语**  
- Child **as** he is, he knows a lot.  
- Much **as** I like it, I won't buy it.  
- Try **as** he might, he failed.

---

**二、部分倒装**  
定义：只把助动词/情态动词/be 提到主语前。  
结构：助动词/情态动词/be + 主语 + 其余部分  
原句无助动词时，用 **do/does/did**。

1. 标志与结构

| 标志 | 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① only + 状语位于句首 | Only + 副词/介词短语/状语从句 + 助动词/be/情态动词 + 主语 + 其余 | Only then **did I** realize.   Only in this way **can we** solve it.   Only when he came back **did I** know. |
| ② 否定意义词/词组位于句首 | 否定词 + 助动词/be/情态动词 + 主语 + 其余 | Never **have I** seen such a film.   Hardly **had he** arrived when it began to rain.   At no time **should you** give up.   Not a word **did he** say. |
| ③ not until 位于句首 | **Not until + 从句/时间状语 + 主句倒装** | Not until he came **did I** leave. |
| ④ so + 助动词 + 主语 | 表示“某人/物也是如此”；只用于肯定句，前后主语不同 | He likes English. So **does she**. |
| ⑤ neither/nor + 助动词 + 主语 | 表示“某人/物也不”；用于否定句，前后主语不同 | He can't swim. Neither **can I**. |
| ⑥ so/such...that 倒装 | So + adj./adv. + be/助动词 + 主语 + that... | So fast **did he** run that nobody caught him. |
| ⑦ 省略 if 的虚拟条件句 | Had/Were/Should + 主语 + 其余 | Had I known, I would have told you.   Were I you, I would go. |
| ⑧ not only...but also | Not only + 助动词 + 主语 + ... but also... | Not only **did he** pass, but he also got high marks. |
| ⑨ may 表祝愿 | May + 主语 + 动词原形 | May you succeed! |


2. 否定倒装常用词  
**never, hardly, seldom, scarcely, little, not until, at no time, not once, not, no, by no means, under no circumstances, on no account, in no case, nowhere** 等。

 三、易错点清单

1. **only 修饰主语时不倒装**：  
    - Only **he** knows the answer. ✅  
    - Only does he know... ❌
2. **only + 状语从句位于句首，倒装主句，不倒装从句**：  
    - Only when he came back **did I** know. ✅  
    - Only when **did he come** back... ❌
3. **not until 倒装主句**：  
    - Not until he came **did I** leave. ✅  
    - Not until **did he come**... ❌
4. **否定词修饰主语时不倒装**：  
    - **No one** knows the answer.  
    - **Not everyone** likes it.
5. **so + 助动词 + 主语 与“确实如此”区分**：  
    - He likes English. **So does she.**（她也是）  
    - —He likes English. —**So he does.**（他确实如此，主语相同）
6. **neither/nor 用于否定句，前后主语不同**。
7. **as/though 让步从句不是完全倒装**，结构是“表语/状语/动词原形 + as/though + 主语 + 谓语”。
8. **there be 句型按就近原则处理更稳**：  
    - There **is** a pen and two books on the desk.

** 四、一句话记忆**

+ **完全倒装**：谓语全提前；副词/地点介词短语开头，代词主语不倒装；表语前置也完全倒装。  
+ **部分倒装**：只提前助动词/be/情态动词；only+状语、否定词、not until、so/neither/nor、so...that、虚拟省略 if、not only 常考。  
+ **特别记**：only 修饰主语不倒装；not until 倒主句；in no time 不倒装；as 让步从句单列；否定词修饰主语不倒装。



# 反义疑问句
前肯后否，前否后肯；特殊的，前面代否定词那些（<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">常见否定词：</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">never, hardly, seldom, scarcely, barely, little, few, nothing, nobody, none, neither, nowhere, no, not, at no time, by no means</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);"> 等</font>），后面用肯；<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">否定前缀不算否定词，句子仍按肯定句处理，后用否定</font>：

带宾语从句的，

1. 主句是 I/We + think/believe/suppose/expect/imagine 等  
反义部分看从句，与从句主语和谓语一致。

I think he is right, isn't he?

We believe she can swim, can't she?

I think he isn't right, is he?

2. 否定转移  
主句是 I/We don't think/believe/suppose/expect... 时，否定转移到从句，反义部分看从句，并用肯定。

I don't think he is right, is he?

We don't believe she can do it, can she?

3. 主句主语不是 I/We，或动词不是上述词  
反义部分看主句，与主句一致。

He thinks she is right, doesn't he?         <font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">He said she was right, </font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">didn't he</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">?</font>

<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">反义疑问句的回答</font>

<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">回答依据</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">事实</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">，不依据反义部分的肯定或否定。</font>

+ <font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">事实是肯定的：</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">Yes + 主语 + 助动词/情态动词</font>**
+ <font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">事实是否定的：</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">No + 主语 + 助动词/情态动词</font>**

<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">并且：</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">Yes 后永远肯定，No 后永远否定。</font>**

1. **<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">否定词算否定</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">：seldom, hardly, few, little, nobody 等，后用肯定。</font>
2. **<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">否定前缀不算否定</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">：unhappy, impossible, dislike 等，后仍用否定。</font>
3. **<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">I/We think/believe/suppose/expect/imagine 看从句</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">；其他情况看主句。</font>
4. **<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">否定转移</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">：I don't think... 反义看从句，用肯定。</font>
5. **<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">Let's → shall we；Let us/其他祈使句 → will you</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">。</font>
6. **<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">there be 按 be 动词和时态</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">，不是固定 are there。</font>
7. **<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">must have done</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">：有明确过去时间→didn't/wasn't；强调完成→hasn't/haven't。</font>
8. **<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">have 的用法要分</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">：有、实义、完成时、have to、had better。</font>
9. **<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">回答看事实</font>**<font style="color:rgb(15, 17, 21);">：Yes 后肯定，No 后否定。</font>

# 感叹句
句尾有感叹号

1，感叹部分是名词性短语的，感叹词用what

考名词可数复数，不可数，what后+不加a，an多，也就是形名相连

2，感叹部分是形容词副词的，感叹句用how，以及形名不相连有a/an，“how 形容词 专有名词”



# 强调句
It is/was+被强调的部分+that/who+其他部分

只要强调部分后用that或who就好了，标志就是it开头，被强调部分可能是一个单词，词组，句子，当强调谓语动词时，在动词前加助动词“do/does/did”



# 祈使句
and连接的表示一个较好的结果

or连接的表示一个不愉快或不理想的结果
